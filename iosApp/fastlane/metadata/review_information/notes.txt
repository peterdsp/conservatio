Conservatio is a documentation tool for conservators of cultural heritage: they register objects, photograph them, mark damage on the photos and export a PDF condition report.

No account is needed to review the app. On the first screen, tap "Continue without an account". Everything is stored on the device.

A full walk-through takes about two minutes:
1. Objects tab: tap + to add an object. Enter a title, add a photo with Camera or Gallery, and tap Save.
2. Open the object, tap the ... button at the top right, then "New Report". Enter any examiner name (the report needs one to save).
3. Under Photos, tap the photo, then tap anywhere on the photo to place a numbered damage marker and choose its type and severity. Go back and tap Save.
4. In the object, tap ... and "Export PDF" to see the PDF condition report with the markers on the photo. The share button sends it to Mail, Files or AirDrop.
5. Settings, Language: switch the app or the report language to Greek, or the report to bilingual Greek and English, and export again.

Settings, "Load sample data" adds two example objects without photos, for a quick look at the lists.

Signing in (Google, Apple or GitHub) is optional. It only connects the app to a Conservatio server for syncing records, and no feature depends on it.

The camera and photo library are used only to add photos of the objects being documented. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
