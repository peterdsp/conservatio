Support

Something not working, or a feature you need for your reports? Write to the developer and you will get an answer from a person, usually within two working days.

Email info@peterdsp.dev

It helps to include your device, the iOS version, and what you did just before the problem. A screenshot is welcome.

Getting started

  1. On the first screen, tap "Continue without an account". You can sign in later from Settings if you want to sync.
  2. In Objects, tap + and register an object with a title and at least one photo.
  3. Open the object, tap the ... button and choose New Report.
  4. Under Photos, open a photo and tap on it to place numbered damage markers.
  5. Save the report, then tap ... and Export PDF to preview and share it.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Everything is saved on your device, so the app works in churches, storerooms and on site without signal.

Can the report be in Greek?
Yes. In Settings, Language, choose the report language: English, Greek, or bilingual Greek and English. The app interface can also be switched to Greek there.

Where is my data?
On your device, unless you sign in to sync. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I delete my account?
Email info@peterdsp.dev from the address you signed in with, and the account and its synced records will be deleted.
