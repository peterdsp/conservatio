Conservatio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: without an account, everything you record in Conservatio stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. If you choose to sign in, your name, email address and the records you sync are stored on the Conservatio server, only to give them back to you. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared.

This policy covers the Conservatio app for iPhone and iPad and the Conservatio web companion. They are made by Petros Dhespollari, who is responsible for the data described here. Questions: info@peterdsp.dev.

Using the app without an account

When you tap "Continue without an account", the objects, photos, condition reports, projects and clients you create are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. They are included in your device backups, as with any app data, and are removed when you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Signing in to sync

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple, Google or GitHub to sync your records with a Conservatio server. When you do:

  • The sign-in provider shares your name and email address with Conservatio. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address. Conservatio never sees your password.
  • The records you sync, including object details, photos and their annotations, are stored on the server so that they are available on your other devices and in the web companion.
  • By default the app connects to the Conservatio server operated by the developer. You can point the app to a server you run yourself under Settings, Sync; your synced data then goes only to that server.

This data is used only to sign you in and to store and return your records. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is not shared with third parties.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera and the photo library only so you can add photos of the objects you document. Photos are saved inside the app and leave your device only if you sync them or share them yourself, for example by exporting a PDF report.

What Conservatio does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Records on your device are kept until you delete them. You can remove them one by one, or all at once under Settings. Synced records and account data are kept while your account exists. To delete your account and everything stored with it on the server, email info@peterdsp.dev from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use, by writing to info@peterdsp.dev. If you are in the European Union you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Conservatio is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
